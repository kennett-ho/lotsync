## 1. What this is

DealerDOH is a dealership operations product **operated by Kennett
Ho** ("the operator" — the resolved v1.1 operating identity, D1; no
separate corporate entity is implied). It is currently a **beta**:
features, data
handling, and availability are still changing, and the product is
offered for internal evaluation and operational use by a
participating dealership — not as a general commercial service.

## 2. Who may use it

Access is by administrator invitation only. You may use DealerDOH
only if you are an authorized member of a participating dealership's
staff, using an account provisioned for you personally. Keep your
credentials to yourself; accounts are individual, and your
dealership's administrators control roles and offboarding.

## 3. Acceptable use

Use DealerDOH for the dealership's own vehicle-operations work.
Do not: attempt to access another dealership's or another person's
data; probe, overload, or circumvent security controls; upload
files you are not authorized to export from the dealership's vendor
systems; or use the service to store data unrelated to its purpose
(in particular, do not upload documents containing customer/consumer
personal or financial information — DealerDOH is not built or
represented for that data; see the Privacy Policy).

## 4. The dealership's data

Operational data processed through DealerDOH originates from the
dealership's own systems and belongs to the dealership. The operator
processes it to provide the service as described in the
[Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) and handles it per
[`SECURITY_OVERVIEW.md`](SECURITY_OVERVIEW.md) and the retention
record ([`DATA_RETENTION.md`](DATA_RETENTION.md)). Disposition of
dealership data at the end of the beta relationship will be agreed
with the dealership ([OWNER/counsel: the commercial data-disposition
process is a recorded v2 prerequisite — do not promise specifics
here that do not exist]).

## 5. Beta expectations (read this one)

- **Availability is not guaranteed.** No SLA, no uptime commitment.
  The service may be interrupted, changed, or paused — including
  free-tier infrastructure behaviors such as cold starts.
- **Features may change or be removed** as the beta evolves.
- **Verify operationally-critical conclusions** against your source
  systems. DealerDOH aggregates and explains evidence from the
  dealership's vendor reports; it does not replace those systems or
  your judgment, and reports can be wrong, late, or incomplete.

## 6. Feedback

Feedback, bug reports, and suggestions are welcome and may be used
to improve the product without obligation or compensation. Feedback
is not confidential unless agreed otherwise.

## 7. Intellectual property

The DealerDOH software, design, and documentation are the
operator's. These terms grant use of the service during the beta,
not any license to the software itself.

## 8. Confidentiality

Beta access may expose non-public product functionality. Treat
product internals — and, always, the dealership's operational data —
as confidential within the dealership's normal duties.

## 9. Suspension and ending

The operator may suspend or end beta access (for an account or the
deployment) for security, misuse, or operational reasons. Your
dealership's administrators may deactivate individual access at any
time; deactivation takes effect immediately.

warranties of any kind, express or implied, to the maximum extent
